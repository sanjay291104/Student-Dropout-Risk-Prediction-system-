Platform

Everything faculty need to act early

A single dashboard connecting prediction, explanation, and intervention — so risk scores turn into real support plans.

Multi-model prediction engine

Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest and XGBoost are trained and benchmarked so the most reliable model always powers predictions.

Early-warning scoring

Attendance, GPA trends, stress index and financial signals are combined into a single, explainable risk percentage per student.

Personalized interventions

Every prediction ships with a ranked action plan — counseling, remedial classes, mentoring or financial aid guidance — tailored to that student's risk factors.

Explainable outputs

Feature-importance breakdowns show faculty exactly which factors are driving each student's risk score — no black box.

Prediction history

Every assessment is logged with a timestamp so mentors can track a student's risk trend across the semester.

Secure faculty accounts

Password-protected logins keep student data restricted to authenticated staff only.

Process

From raw data to a support plan in four steps

1

Collect signals

Attendance, GPA, backlogs, stress index and financial data are entered or imported per student.

2

Preprocess & encode

Missing values are imputed, categorical fields encoded, and features scaled for the model.

3

Predict risk

The best-performing trained model (Random Forest / XGBoost) scores the student's dropout probability.

4

Recommend action

Rule-based logic maps risk drivers to concrete interventions faculty can act on immediately.
