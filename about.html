Student Dropout Risk Prediction & Intervention Recommendation System

A machine-learning early-warning system that goes beyond binary dropout classification — pairing every prediction with an explainable, personalized intervention plan for faculty.

Abstract

Student dropout is a major challenge in higher education that affects both students and institutions. Existing studies mainly focus on predicting dropout using academic data and machine learning models, with limited focus on intervention and decision support. This project uses student-related factors — academic performance, attendance, and engagement data — to train Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and XGBoost models, comparing them on accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score. The system goes further than prediction: it generates explainable, personalized intervention recommendations so institutions can take timely action to improve student retention.

Limitations of existing systems

✕Binary classification only — no further guidance for faculty.
✕Focused on prediction rather than prevention.
✕No personalized intervention strategies per student.
✕Struggles with complex nonlinear relationships in data.
✕Requires manual follow-up by faculty after prediction.
